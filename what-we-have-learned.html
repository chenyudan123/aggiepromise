The AggiePromise Project asks two connected questions. Each finding below is tied to one of them, with a link to the full paper or brief when one is available.

Employment Trajectories

What patterns of employment do youth with disabilities follow in the 18 months after leaving high school, and what personal, family, and workplace factors distinguish successful pathways?

Lived Experience

What is the first stretch of the school-to-work transition like for youth themselves: the barriers they meet, the supports that help, and the choices they make?
